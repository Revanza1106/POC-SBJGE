Our Principal Partner

FulSailing, the engineering team behind our equipment.

Chengdu FulSailing Energy Technology (FSL) designs and builds drilling and completion tools in Chengdu, China. Since 2021 it has grown from a small R&D team into a manufacturer with its own machining, assembly and field service crews.

We bring FSL's tools and specialists to projects in Indonesia, and handle the local side: sourcing, import, delivery and on-site support.

What FSL builds

The main tools and materials we can supply and support in Indonesia.

  • Hydro ThrusterAxial vibration that cuts drag and keeps weight on bit in deviated and horizontal wells. A zero-pressure-drop version suits rigs with limited pump pressure.
  • Near-Bit Azimuthal Gamma SubReads gamma, inclination and vibration right behind the bit and sends it to surface in real time for better geosteering.
  • Multiple Activation Bypass ToolOpens and closes bypass ports downhole for hole cleaning or pumping LCM, without pulling out of hole.
  • Repeatable Pressure-Test Burst SystemCreates the first frac stage opening and allows two casing pressure tests before fracturing.
  • Continuous Circulation Drilling SystemKeeps mud moving during connections for stable bottom-hole pressure and cleaner holes.
  • Floatation Collar & Cement Support UmbrellaHelps casing reach depth in long horizontal wells, and gives cement plugs a firm base.
  • Casing Running Tool & Liner HangerMechanical top drive CRT for 5" to 20" casing, and hydraulic-set liner hangers rated over 380 tons.
  • Loss Prevention & Plugging MaterialsWater- and oil-based materials for fractured, porous and high-pressure loss zones, plus engineering on site.

Certified: ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 (Bureau Veritas) · API Spec Q1, with API 7-1 and API 11D1 monograms for Kelly valves and packers.
